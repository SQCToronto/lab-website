title: Non-Markovian Relaxation Spectroscopy of Fluxonium Qubits
date: 2026-02-24
category: paper
summary: "A qubit does not relax alone: its environment has dynamics of its own. This Nature Communications study uses two-timescale relaxometry to probe both across roughly 0.1 to 0.4 GHz. The measurements reveal a discrete spectrum of two-level systems with millisecond lifetimes, consistent with defects in the aluminum-oxide tunnel barriers of a fluxonium junction chain. The result turns relaxation into a useful spectroscopic tool for understanding microscopic loss in superconducting circuits."
