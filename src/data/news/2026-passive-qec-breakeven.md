title: Passive Quantum Error Correction of Photon Loss at Breakeven
date: 2026-05-22
category: paper
summary: Photon loss is a central challenge for quantum memories. In a steady-state, driven-dissipative system built from a superconducting cavity and transmon ancilla, the team protects a binomially encoded logical qubit without measurement-based feedback. The resulting logical coherence exceeds the photon-lifetime limit by about 5 percent. Published in Physical Review X, the experiment demonstrates continuous passive correction operating at breakeven and offers a practical route toward protecting fragile quantum information with engineered dissipation.
